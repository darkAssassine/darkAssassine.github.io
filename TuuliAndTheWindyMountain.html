Tuuli And The Windy Mountain

Godot, GDScript

1. Semester Itch.io

Summary

Tuuli and the Windy Mountain is a charming 2.5D platformer following a young wind spirit's journey up a mystical mountain. This was a foundational project where I learned programming basics and the Godot engine. I implemented platformer mechanics including "koyote time" to create more forgiving and enjoyable player controls. The project taught me the value of starting with simple concepts before expanding, and how thoughtful constraints often lead to better game design.
